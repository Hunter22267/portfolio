# AI-Focused Technical Writing Portfolio

I'm developing my skills in technical writing, with a focus on AI tools, prompting workflows, and documentation that makes technical concepts easier to understand and use.

This repository contains my writing samples and documents my progress toward creating clear, useful, and reliable technical documentation.

## Writing Samples

### [How to Fix Headphones That Have No Sound in Windows 11](headphones-troubleshooting.md)

**Type:** Troubleshooting guide

A step-by-step guide to diagnosing and resolving common Windows 11 headphone audio problems. This sample demonstrates user-focused instructions, structured troubleshooting, and the organization of technical information for a general audience.

## Areas of Focus

- **AI documentation:** Explaining AI tools, features, and workflows in clear, practical language.
- **Prompt design and evaluation:** Documenting prompt development, testing methods, and iterative improvements.
- **Technical communication:** Turning complex processes into accurate, accessible, and actionable instructions.

## Approach

My goal is to produce documentation that is useful to its intended audience, grounded in reliable information, and organized around the task the reader needs to accomplish.

For AI-focused projects, I aim to go beyond describing prompts or outputs by documenting the reasoning, testing, limitations, and results behind a workflow.
